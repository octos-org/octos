## Why the original plan would not have worked

The starting runbook expected a `git merge-base --is-ancestor` sweep to find
200–300 dead branches. In reality **zero** deletable branches are ancestors of
`main`. The backlog is squash/rebase-merged: the commits are gone, but every
*change* is already in `main`. A sweep that only checked ancestry would have
deleted nothing while 443 dead branches stayed.

So this audit reports a second category — **patch-equivalent**, detected with
`git cherry` (no commit unique to the branch still applies). 443 of the 732
branches fall here, and they are genuinely safe to delete.

## Branches are not judged by age alone

The 163 branches idle for more than 3 months are reported, **not** deleted
automatically: they may hold unmerged work. Only ancestor/patch-equivalent
branches are swept by
[`.github/workflows/cleanup-stale-branches.yml`](../../.github/workflows/cleanup-stale-branches.yml).
The rest are a human decision.
